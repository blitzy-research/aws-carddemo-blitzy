Most of the accessibility work on this site is done in docs/stylesheets/extra.css, because a stylesheet is the one mechanism that survives every publication path unchanged. Five things, though, are attributes rather than presentation - an accessible name, aria-modal, aria-current, a scope, a tabindex that must be REMOVED - and no stylesheet can add or remove an attribute. They are done here. WHY NOT SCRIPT. The obvious alternative was a small script.
