1. What was migrated

The whole estate, not a representative subset. Every program, every copybook and every job member was read; one copybook was deliberately excluded and the exclusion is recorded as a decision.

28COBOL programs — 17 online, 10 batch, 1 shared subprogram
19,254lines of COBOL source
28copybooks, two of them procedural
17BMS mapsets and their 17 symbolic maps
29JCL members plus 2 cataloged procedures
544procedure units, every one traced

The 544 is 528 paragraphs in the programs' procedure divisions plus 14 from the date-validation copybook and 2 from the function-key copybook. Each has a row in the traceability matrix naming its source member, its line, its target class, its target method and the test that covers it.

The legacy tree was never copied and never edited. The Java module is physically disjoint at carddemo-java/; app/ stays byte-identical because it is simultaneously the parity baseline for every golden fixture and the anchor every traceability row cites. Traceability is by citation, never by transcription.

3. Faithful beats idiomatic

Wherever COBOL semantics and natural Java diverged, COBOL won and the divergence was written down. These are the decisions that would have compiled cleanly, read plausibly and been wrong.

Arithmetic truncates, because nothing in the estate rounds

The keyword ROUNDED appears zero times in 19,254 lines. A COBOL store without it truncates toward zero, so every monetary scale uses RoundingMode.DOWN. The conventional HALF_EVEN would differ by one cent on roughly half of all interest computations — invisible to a test written under the same wrong assumption, and a byte-parity failure.

Expression order is preserved operand for operand

( TRAN-CAT-BAL * DIS-INT-RATE) / 1200 multiplies first. Dividing the rate by 1200 first is algebraically identical and moves the truncation point, so it is prohibited.

Defects are reproduced, not repaired

  • An empty fee paragraph is genuinely invoked and implements nothing. It survives as a documented no-op. Inventing fee logic would be feature expansion and would change interest output.
  • The end-of-file control break is dead code: the last account's interest records are written while its balance and cycle accumulators are never updated. Both halves are reproduced — the final group accrues and emits, and its account is not rewritten.
  • Two account-update fields are decorated for error display but never validated. The DTO adds no constraint to them, because that would reject input the legacy accepts.
  • An alphabetic check that blanks letters and trims passes embedded spaces. The faithful predicate is "letter or space", not allMatch(isLetter).
  • Fourteen source anomalies — misspelled field names, duplicated labels, a conflicting generation limit — are recorded in the register and propagated into no new logic.
Two deliberate departures, both labelled as improvements rather than translations. The legacy compares an eight-character plaintext password directly; the target stores a BCrypt hash. Every legacy file is defined with uncommitted reads, no recovery and no journal; the target runs PostgreSQL READ COMMITTED with JPA @Version. Both are stronger than the baseline, and saying so in the log is what stops a reviewer reading the stronger posture as a regression.
